Methodology

Annual growth is based on weighted 10Y/8Y/6Y/4Y/2Y CAGR with default weights 10/15/20/25/30%, followed by a 20% recent-trend adjustment. The supplied historical workbook contains annual State/UT data, not historical State/UT month-wise data. Therefore, the current monthly profile is a transparent planning profile. Once historical monthly State/UT data are supplied, the tool can replace this with state-specific historical seasonality and weather sensitivity.
