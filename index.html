Model Methodology

1. Energy Requirement: Forecast is generated month-wise. For each month, CAGR is calculated over 10Y, 8Y, 6Y, 4Y and 2Y historical periods. The user can set the weights for 10Y, 8Y, 6Y, 4Y and 2Y CAGR. The tool normalizes the weights automatically if they do not total 100%.

2. Recent trend: A limited recent YoY adjustment is applied to avoid one unusual year dominating the projection. The adjustment is capped to maintain stability.

3. Peak Demand: Peak demand is never added. Each month's peak is forecast independently and the annual peak is the maximum of the 12 monthly forecasts.

4. Weather: Weather is treated as a secondary correction to the trend forecast, not as an additional growth factor. The All-India monthly temperature and rainfall series are applied uniformly to each State/UT. Historical State-wise relationships with weather anomalies determine only the direction and relative strength of the correction. The normal-weather forecast is therefore driven primarily by demand trends.

5. Weather cap: Under the Normal scenario, the weather correction is capped at ±2% for Energy Requirement and ±3% for Peak Demand. Temperature has 75% of the weather-signal weight and rainfall 25%. Warm/Hot/Cool/Dry/Wet scenarios add only a small incremental stress. This prevents weather data from causing an unrealistically high forecast.

6. Forecast year: The tool is restricted to FY 2027-28. The historical data in Dataset.xlsx are used to generate this single forecast year.

7. Weather interpretation: Temperature and rainfall are explanatory variables, not additive demand. The weather effect is limited to prevent unrealistic projections.

8. Data source: Dataset.xlsx supplied by the user, containing FY-wise State/UT Energy Requirement and Peak Demand and All-India monthly temperature/rainfall series.
